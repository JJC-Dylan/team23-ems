Demonstration accounts

The URS records the assumption that login accounts are created outside this application. These three demonstration accounts are seeded so the role behaviour can be shown:

RoleUsernamePasswordMay do
HR hrhr123 Add, edit, delete employees; view all shifts
Supervisor supervisorsuper123 Assign shifts; read-only employee data
Ordinary Employee employeeemp123 View only their own work shifts

Three fixed daily shifts

  • Morning
  • Afternoon
  • Evening

Role-based access

  • HR owns the employee-record lifecycle.
  • Supervisor assigns the daily shifts.
  • Ordinary Employee sees only their own schedule.

Internal shop-administration tool. Employee data and shift assignments persist in a local SQLite database.
